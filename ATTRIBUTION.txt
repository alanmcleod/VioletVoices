Violet Voices piano samples

Derived from Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm.
Original instrument: Yamaha C5 grand piano.
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0).

Changes made for Violet Voices:
- selected genuine soft (v3) and loud (v13) velocity recordings
- generated a chromatic C2-C6 set from the nearest recorded minor-third sample
- pitch-corrected with high-quality offline processing
- trimmed leading silence while retaining the hammer attack
- level-balanced, shortened and gently faded at the tail
- encoded as Ogg Vorbis for browser playback
